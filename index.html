The Strategic Shift
In the Twin Cities, the sport
still has to
introduce itself.
Soccer is the default sport everywhere else in the world — woven into culture, community, and identity. In Minnesota, there isn’t that default yet. Parents here didn’t grow up with it. There’s no assumed shared language around the game. That’s not a liability. For Salvo, it’s the opportunity. Building excitement for the sport itself — not just enrollment in a club — is part of the work. And Salvo can be the organization to do it.

Content Strategy
Our Approach to Social.
Every piece of content we create answers to at least one of three goals.
Six Content Categories
  • Education
    Technique breakdowns. Coaching philosophy explainers. The “why” behind what Salvo does. Genuinely useful content that parents and players actually want to watch.
  • Authenticity
    Content that speaks to the real soccer community — the global game, the culture, the passion. Salvo’s story lives in those spaces.
  • Storytelling
    Player journeys. Families who found a home at Salvo. Coaches who grew up in the same communities they now serve. Financial aid recipients whose lives changed because of access.
  • Highlight Reels
    High-energy, music-driven game highlights and training clips. The kind of content a 14-year-old wants to share because it makes them look good.
  • Viral Trends
    Trending audio, formats, and challenges adapted to Salvo’s world. We won’t chase every trend — only the ones that fit the brand. When they fit, we move fast.
  • UGC
    Parents, volunteers, players, and coaches are already creating content. We build a simple pipeline — prompts, permissions, submission process — so the community becomes a content engine.
